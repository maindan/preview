Where your donation goes

Why donate?

ALFA depends on continued support to maintain rare and essential research infrastructure for understanding the Amazon rainforest.

  • 01

    Fund research on biodiversity and forest fragmentation.

  • 02

    Support field logistics, monitoring, and maintenance of study areas.

  • 03

    Train students, early-career researchers, and local field teams.

Tax-deductible donations for U.S. citizens

U.S. citizens can make tax-deductible donations to BDFFP through the Smithsonian Tropical Research Institution (STRI). Please include "Donation to BDFFP, Manaus, Brazil" in the comments.
