Why ALFA

The structure behind the BDFFP, working for your project

For almost four decades ALFA has managed the funds, technical staff and logistics of the world's largest forest fragmentation experiment. Everything we do for the BDFFP every day is available to other initiatives.

We take on the administrative and financial management of research, conservation and outreach projects for an administrative fee that covers the cost of the service, with clear processes and funder-ready reports.

  • Since 1986

    Hands-on experience managing long-term projects, Brazilian and international grants, and field operations in the forest.

  • Our own system

    Every routine runs on ALFA's management system: each transaction, document and trip is recorded and traceable.

  • Sound accounts

    An audit committee, accounting reports and supporting documents organized for audits and funder reporting.

  • Boots on the ground

    Staff, vehicles, field stations and camps in the Amazon, with planning and costs known before every trip.

What we offer

Our services

Hire a single service or full project management. ALFA already runs each of these areas on its management system.

01

Project financial management

We execute your project budget line by line, from receiving the funds to the last payment.

  • Bank accounts and statement reconciliation
  • Payments and receipts by budget line
  • Budget reallocations with full history
  • Supplier and expense category tracking
02

Financial reporting

Reports ready for funders, boards and audits, with receipts linked to every expense.

  • Summaries by project, line, category and period
  • General accounting report in PDF and Excel
  • Accounting spreadsheets for your accountants
  • Documents and invoices attached to each entry
03

Field logistics

Planning and running field trips, with schedules, transport and camps organized.

  • Field trip scheduling by project and person
  • Trips, vehicles and drivers
  • Occupancy calendar for stations and camps
  • Field cost estimates for each expedition
04

HR and payroll

Hiring and managing your project staff under Brazilian labor law, just as ALFA does with its own technical team.

  • Staff records linked to projects
  • Payroll and pay slips
  • Income tax withholding, extras and deductions
  • Field per diems for drivers and assistants
05

Project and proposal management

From receiving the proposal to following the project as it runs, all in one place.

  • Project proposals and grant requests
  • Review in meetings with recorded agendas
  • Team, institutions and funding for each project
  • Required documents and project history
06

Scientific output

Organizing and sharing your project's output, to show results to funders and the public.

  • Publications, authors and journals
  • Numbering for technical series
  • Public publications repository
  • Output indicators and charts
07

Governance and transparency

Recorded decision-making and controlled access, with open data whenever the project chooses to share it.

  • Meetings, agendas and decisions on record
  • Access profiles by role and project
  • History of permission changes
  • Public indicators dashboard
08

Assets and investments

Tracking the equipment bought with project funds and following invested balances.

  • Asset and equipment inventory
  • Depreciation calculation
  • Investment accounts and returns
  • Balance forecasts under different scenarios
09

Online fundraising

Online donations for research and conservation initiatives, recorded automatically.

  • One-time gifts by card or Pix
  • Monthly card donations
  • Donations in reais and US dollars
  • Every donation recorded and searchable
ALFA platform

Everything tracked on our own management system

Project leads and teams can check progress at any time, without scattered spreadsheets or long email threads.

  • Role-based access

    Each person sees and does only what their role allows.

  • Up-to-date balances

    Budget execution by line, available at any time.

  • Centralized documents

    Receipts, contracts and reports linked to the right record.

  • PDF and Excel reports

    Exports ready for funders and accountants.

  • Traceability

How it works

From first contact to final report

A simple process, with responsibilities and costs defined from day one.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    We learn about the project, the funder, the timeline and what needs managing.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    We send the scope of services and the corresponding administrative fee.

  3. 03

    Setup

    We open the accounts, load the budget by line and give your team access.

  4. 04

    Ongoing management

    We run the routines and deliver reports on the funder's schedule.
